# Melody-table permissions

`haftarah.json` and `torah.json` contain the Binder/Avery teaching-source
transcriptions and variants identified in their source fields and accompanying
research. On 2026-10-15 Daniel reported permission for their **noncommercial use**
and expressly confirmed that these tables may be included in this **public
repository**. This records the permission he communicated; it does not grant
unrestricted commercial use or sublicensing, or assign a Creative Commons
license. These tables are separate from the repository's MIT-licensed code.
Comparison against the printed source remains an accuracy task, not an
unresolved permission request for this authorized publication.

`haftarah-moroccan.json` transcribes A. Z. Idelsohn, *Thesaurus of Oriental Hebrew
Melodies*, volume V, *Songs of the Moroccan Jews*, nos. 271-272, p. 103, published
by Benjamin Harz in 1928. The [original scan](https://archive.org/details/31761125265157)
and its [title-page text](https://archive.org/download/31761125265157/31761125265157_djvu.txt)
identify that volume and year. The original 1928 edition is in the US public
domain after expiration of its publication term; see the US Copyright Office's
[duration guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf). This refers to
the original notation, not later editions or performances. Its table/fixture
source references preserve the attribution and transcription choices.
