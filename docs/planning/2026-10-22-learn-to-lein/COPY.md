# Learn to lein: shared-surface copy

2026-10-23. Final copy for the surfaces outside the lessons. Lesson text is in lessons.json. Sentence case, plain verbs, no exclamation points, adult-respectful; the same words serve a 12-year-old and a 60-year-old.

## Front door: the Learn to lein card

Title: Learn to lein
Line: Hear the phrases, then lein a verse of this week's reading.

## Course home

Intro (two lines):
About ten short phrases make up the whole tune. Hear them one at a time, then lein a verse.

Links: Trope chart · About

## Review

Due line on the course home: Review: 5 phrases, about 2 minutes
Nothing due: (no line; the next lesson stands alone)
Before any lesson is done, if the learner opens Review: Nothing to review yet. Finish a lesson and its phrases come back here.
End of a review run: Done. These come back on Thursday.  (the day is computed; if more than a week away: "These come back next week.")

## Lesson screens

Hear it: the family name, the "listen for" sentence from lessons.json, and the play tile labelled Hear it.
Hear it in verses: heading "In verses"; each row shows the reference and a play tile.
Drill prompts (one line each):
- Hear and name: Which phrase is this?
- Same or different: Same tune or different?  Buttons: Same tune · Different tune
- Tap the phrase you heard: Hear a phrase, then tap it.
- Where does the phrase end: Tap the word where the phrase ends.
- Build the phrase: Hear it, then build it.
- In this week's reading: heading "In this week's reading"; prompt "Find three more."; done line "3 of 3 found."
Feedback lines:
- Right: the chip or phrase takes its colour; no text beyond the family name.
- Wrong: That one is [family]. Here is [chosen family] for comparison.  (then both play)
- Re-ask: (no text; the item simply returns)
End of lesson:
- Heading: the lesson title, then "Done"
- Body: the lesson's goal sentence, rewritten in the present ("You can hear etnachta: the rest in the middle of the verse.")
- Next: Next: Zakef  · Button: Start next lesson · Link: Back to the path

## This week's reading, special cases

- Text-only haftarah: No recording for this haftarah yet.
- No reading this week (festival weekday): Showing the coming Shabbat.
- No word-ready verse at all (should not happen for Torah): the fallback examples in lessons.json are used, with the line "From the course's own verses while this week's recording is not ready."

## Empty and error states

- Audio failed to load: The recording did not load. Try again.
- Offline (signed-in learner, installed app): Lessons you have opened work offline. This week's reading needs a connection.
- Signed out, progress note on the course home (one line, muted): Progress is saved on this device. Sign in to keep it.

## About

Learn to lein uses human recordings from PocketTorah (CC BY-SA), cut by word, and the text of Miqra according to the Masorah via Sefaria (CC BY-SA). The melodies are the Standard Reform cantillation of Binder and Avery. Your cantor's or tutor's version of a phrase wins over the recording's.
