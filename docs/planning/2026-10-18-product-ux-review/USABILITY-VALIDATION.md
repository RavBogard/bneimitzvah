# How to establish that the product is intuitive

Consultant evaluation plan • 18 October 2026 • Proposed criteria; none of the task times or success rates below has been measured.

An expert source review can identify unnecessary decisions and inconsistent behavior. It cannot establish that an unfamiliar person will understand the interface. This review covers the product systematically; authenticated live tasks, actual iPhone behavior and novice comprehension remain separate evidence needs.

Daniel is the only human tester permitted during development. Do not recruit families, tutors or students until the revised 1.0 is feature complete and Daniel approves that testing. This document neither initiates testing nor authorizes use of real private records.

## Standard and method

Ask the person to complete a goal in ordinary language. Do not name the button or narrate the navigation. Record whether they finish unaided, where they hesitate, whether they choose the wrong person or action, and whether they can explain the final state. A successful task includes knowing what happened.

Use approved synthetic scenarios for administrative changes and invitations; no accidental mail, grant or real recipient link should result from an evaluation. Real-source listening and activation checks follow their existing separate approval process. Retain anonymized observations, not family records, personal content or secret links.

Suggested targets for later permitted evaluation:

- At least four of five representative participants complete each common routine task without a hint. A small sample is formative evidence, not a population estimate.
- No wrong-person action, misunderstood access scope or silent loss of work in observed critical scenarios. One such failure warrants review regardless of average task speed.
- A returning learner can locate their assigned reading and identify the main available practice action within 15 seconds. A parent can identify the selected child and current mode without searching.
- A routine assignment using an already-known date/service can be chosen, reviewed and saved within two minutes after staff knows which passage they want. Separate time spent making a teaching choice from time spent operating forms.
- Immediately after saving, inviting or creating a link, the user can explain what changed, who can see it, and whether anything was sent.

These are proposed product targets, not evidence-backed current baselines or immutable contractual limits. Revise timings if Daniel's first observed baseline reveals a better meaningful target; retain the comprehension and safety criteria.

## Interruption and accessibility checks

Apply these checks to the major journeys rather than treating them as a separate cosmetic pass:

| Condition | What to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Actual iPhone Safari | Keyboard covering fields/actions, focus and zoom behavior, native selection, browser Back, safe-area space, audio start/stop, lock/app switching, links returning from email. A 390×844 Chrome viewport does not establish these. |
| Small screen and larger text | Hebrew marks remain legible, English/Hebrew direction remains correct, controls and status remain reachable without horizontal page scrolling. Focused reading can be enlarged without hiding necessary actions. |
| Keyboard and VoiceOver | Logical focus order, visible focus, descriptive names, appropriate selected/expanded states, usable dialog entry/return, status announcements, understandable mixed-direction reading. No pass is claimed by this audit. |
| Color and themes | Trope/progress meaning has non-color identifiers; light/dark text and controls remain readable. Check actual contrast and mark clarity; CSS token counts are not a contrast assessment. |
| Slow or interrupted connection | Loading, empty, unavailable and not-authorized states differ. Existing data does not disappear misleadingly during a refresh. Unknown save outcomes give a safe next step. |
| Departure and return | Controlled close/Back protects changed forms; foreground return retains allowed work; genuine authority loss clears private content. Check actual behavior rather than promising durable unsaved drafts. |
| Desktop/tablet teaching | Passage identifiers and tools can be discussed over Zoom; opening notes does not make the shared reading illegible or move to the wrong student. |
| Partial setup | No reading, one reading only, missing English/audio, no lesson, incomplete honors and no role each give a helpful next step without pretending content exists. |

Privacy and recovery sometimes restrict seamless resumption. The requirement is understandable, safe recovery, not weakening the boundary to make the task appear faster.

## Record and decision

For each scenario retain: build/date/device, participant role category, synthetic scenario, outcome, assistance needed, confusing wording, observed failures, participant's explanation of the final state, and disposition. Use Pass / Needs revision / Blocked by unavailable capability, with evidence. Do not average away a failed access or wrong-person scenario.
