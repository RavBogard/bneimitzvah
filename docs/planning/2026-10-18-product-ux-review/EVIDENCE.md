## What still needs real product research

These are practical evidence needs for planning, not requests to conduct engineering or recruit testers now. Daniel remains the only human tester until the mission's release/testing conditions allow otherwise.

1. **Baseline a few real phone tasks with Daniel.** Can he return to the right child's reading, identify what to practice, prepare the next lesson, invite the right person and complete one family-honors task without interpreting the system? Observe sequence, hesitation and confidence, not just whether a screen renders.
2. **Validate the no-login recipient's actual context.** Reopening an original blessing link after switching apps is a meaningful phone behavior because the guest surface deliberately clears on concealment. Establish the guidance and recovery the invited recipient needs while preserving its privacy contract. Real material activation is a separate prerequisite.
3. **Confirm the vocabulary.** Check whether families recognize aliyah, span/division, practice view and service slot, and whether tutors/admins interpret contact, invitation, role and relationship correctly. A label's existence in source is not proof it matches the user's mental model.
4. **Observe empty and partial experiences.** A student with one reading, no service parts, missing English or unavailable reference audio needs a coherent next action. Synthetic content and success screenshots do not establish that coherence.
5. **Check interruption and resumption as a person experiences them.** Email switching, phone lock, tab return, an uncertain save and a changed ceremony are ordinary situations. The product must communicate what was saved, what remains only on this device/tab and which safe step to take next.
6. **Confirm operational reality before naming integrations as features.** Determine who owns the roster, which stable IDs exist, what staff are expected to do with import mismatches, and the real available service/source catalogs. No live provider write or data import is authorized by this documentation.
7. **Resolve held scope explicitly.** Student audio acceptance, real blessing material activation, adult honors self-edit, settings/onboarding, richer translation/reference tools, tikkun/printing/drills and integration workflows should each have a product owner and an honest user-visible availability decision. Parked recordings/generated chant and post1.0 extras should not crowd current navigation.
