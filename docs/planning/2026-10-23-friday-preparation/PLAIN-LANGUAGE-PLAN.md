# CRC Torah plan in plain language

The everyday job is to open a portion, explore it, choose a reading, understand it, prepare to lein or drash, and print what is useful. People can do this in the app itself or work with Claude. Custom readings are common. A custom guide from a rabbi is an occasional addition.

## The main experience

1. **Open the right reading.** Start with the whole week's parashah. A link from Claude can open the selected verses directly in Study.
2. **Choose and save any verses.** Choose while reading or enter an exact passage. Allow separated groups. Move easily between the selected verses and their place in the whole portion. Custom readings should work equally well for learning to lein and preparing to lein or drash.
3. **Explore naturally in the app.** Read Hebrew and English, open Rashi and other commentators, follow related sources, and return to the passage. This works without preparing anything with Claude first.
4. **Make word taps match the activity.** Study opens a word's meaning and offers grammar. Lein hears the reading by default. Both activities retain access to the other tools.
5. **Make original commentary easy to read.** Show English with clear source and translator credit. Open a source in full close to its verse. Keep the current reference visible and return to the same place when the source closes.
6. **Let Claude work with preparation too.** Claude can help choose and reopen readings, explore sources with the person, and add important translations through MCP. Access follows the person's own authority. Thinking and drafting stay in Claude; cut the app's personal writing fields from the normal reader.
7. **Print cleanly.** Reuse the print feature already being built. Print the selected Hebrew and English. Optional selected original commentary goes on pages afterward.
8. **Keep navigation quiet and useful.** Show the chapter and verse, offer easy jumps, and preserve place across tools and reading views. Keep the existing visual direction. A quieter initial color setting in Study is a design recommendation.

## Occasional study with a rabbi

9. **Add a curated discussion through MCP when it is useful.** The rabbi names a question or theme and works with Claude to choose original sources. Saying to add it publishes it for everyone. The app displays it beside the relevant verses and through an optional question list when such material exists. No introductions, connecting essays, normal-app guide editor, or required question-building workflow.
10. **Keep existing student guides secondary.** They remain available when useful and do not determine how ordinary reading or exploration works.

## UI inspiration

From [Sefaria](https://www.sefaria.org/Genesis.12?lang=bi&with=Rashi), borrow the clear relationship between a selected verse and its source panel, and the route from a verse to related texts.

From the [haftarah reader](https://haftarah.centralreform.org/2026-10-17-noach), borrow the nearby word/source panels, plain-language word explanation before deeper dictionary detail, full-source expansion, and dependable return to the reading. Keep CRC Torah's own visual direction and confirmed English commentary presentation.

## Suggested order

First make common custom readings and independent exploration smooth, including the different word actions in Study and Lein. Connect that experience to Claude and the existing print work. Add the occasional guided-study support after the ordinary experience is coherent.
