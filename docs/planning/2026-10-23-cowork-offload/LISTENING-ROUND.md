# Learn to lein: listening round

For Daniel, about 10 minutes. On each row, open the link, tap **Hear it** (the first screen of the lesson), and mark two answers. The clip is the lesson's first example, cut from the PocketTorah recording.

**Say it like this.** A clip "cuts a word" (y) if any of these happen:
- **start cut:** the first word starts late, so its first sound or syllable is missing;
- **end cut:** the last word stops before its final syllable or before its last note settles;
- **extra before / extra after:** you hear part of the word before or after the phrase.

Anything else (a breath, a quiet gap, a slightly long tail of silence) is not a cut: mark n. In the notes, write which of the four it was and on which word, for example "end cut, שָׁלֽוֹם". For the second answer, mark y if the bold "listen for" line describes what the reader sings on this clip; if not, write in a few words what the reader does instead.

**Pacing.** About 25 seconds a row. Row 11 by minute 5, row 21 by minute 10. If a clip will not load, write "no load" and move on.

**Why 22 rows.** There are 21 family lessons (10 Torah, 11 haftarah). The PR #33 entry in STATE.md says 22 clips because the intro lesson, How a verse is built, also opens with a Hear it (the whole of Genesis 1:1). It is row 0 here. The two Your first verse lessons have no Hear it screen and are not in the round.

Links are on beitmitzvah.centralreform.org; after the switch, the same paths work on torah.centralreform.org. Example words are the JSON word range in lessons.json (counting from 0).
