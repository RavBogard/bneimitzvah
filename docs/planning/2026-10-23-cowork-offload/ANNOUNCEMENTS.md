## 1. Congregation announcement (eblast or website)

**Subject:** CRC Torah: read, study and learn to lein

CRC Torah is a free reading site for this week's Torah and haftarah, at torah.centralreform.org [address live after the switch]. You do not need an account to use it.

**Lein.** Read this Shabbat's portion with the trope colored in, and play any verse from a recording at your own speed.

**Study.** Open the same text with Rashi and other commentary beside each verse, compare translations, and see more on any verse.

**Learn to lein.** A short course, three to five minutes a lesson, that starts with how a verse is built and ends with you chanting one verse of this week's reading. It works on a phone, with no sign-up and no score.

**Your own account.** An account keeps your own readings, translation and notes, and they stay private. Everything else on CRC Torah is open without one. To ask for one, open the site, choose Ask for an account, and give your name. You will get one email when it is approved.

## 2. Note for tutors and approvers

Subject: Approving CRC Torah accounts

When someone asks for an account, each approver gets one short email saying a request is waiting. It carries no name or message. Open Accounts in the workspace (/workspace/accounts) to see the request: the person's name, what they would like to work on, their verified email address and the date. CRC Torah is open to anyone, member or not, so approve anyone who asks in good faith. Decline only requests that look like spam or abuse. Approve gives the person an account at once and sends them one email telling them it is ready. Decline removes the request and sends nothing. Approving gives the person a place to keep their own readings; it does not give them any other access, and no one on staff can read what they save. Admins approve by default; an admin can add a tutor as an approver and can end a membership. If a request looks wrong or you are unsure, ask Daniel before approving.
