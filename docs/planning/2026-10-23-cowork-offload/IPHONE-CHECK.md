# iPhone check of Learn to lein, about 20 minutes

For a tutor or a family member, at the pilot, on a real iPhone. Daniel names the moment; nothing here goes to families before then. Use Safari first, then the Home Screen app in Part D. Write down the iPhone model, iOS version and the date first: ______________________

Mark each step Pass or Fail. Put anything odd in the note column, with the step number. Parts A to C work signed out. Part D needs you signed in. If a step fails, take a screenshot and keep going. Print on one sheet, front and back. Record the result in STATE afterwards.

Before you start: set the ring/silent switch (the small switch above the volume buttons) to ring, and turn the volume up. Part B asks you to test it on silent too.
