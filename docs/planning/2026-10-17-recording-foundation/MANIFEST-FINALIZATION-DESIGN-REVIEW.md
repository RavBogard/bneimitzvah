## Original findings and incorporated corrections

The earlier independent ignored review accepted the direction with six required precisions; it did not accept the first draft as implementation-ready. The public spec now incorporates them and the subsequent root choices:

1. **Durable consumption and final-state SQL.** Reciprocal exact receipt/ledger links and canonical facts never clear after consumption. Receiving/verifying are unconsumed; registered requires complete consumption; retiring/terminal permit either irreversibly consumed or unconsumed history. Deferred checks inspect both final row sets, complete nonempty slot cardinality/index/length sum and explicit NULL refusal. Immutable seal tuples are distinct from later lifecycle/claim/date revisions, which invalidate tickets without destroying valid consumed linkage.
2. **Release cannot be forged on INSERT.** Existing0020/0021 table-wide runtime INSERT privileges mean withholding UPDATE alone was insufficient. Every new physical-release/retirement-confirmed fact starts NOT NULL/default false with INSERT-time refusal of true, a guarded monotone transition and no runtime role/GUC spoof. Complete terminal evidence, not vacuous NOT EXISTS or state text, gates worker-only release. Production maintenance credentials/scheduling remain absent.
3. **Net-zero transfer and separate physical release.** One exact reciprocal predicate selects receipt charge before consumption and linked-ledger charge afterward. Consumption excludes only its reservation and does not set physical-release bits. All other/unlinked/deleting liabilities remain additive. Only the separate worker fact can remove consumed linked-ledger audio/take charge after complete terminal evidence; permanent ID union, slot markers and metadata remain. This prevents double charging and premature release without needing a skip-capacity flag.
4. **Monotone attempts and unresolved faults.** P/U and claim budgets are spent before positively acknowledged outer-COMMIT permissions; failed/unknown ACK yields no dispatch. Replacement claims, sessions, dates or retiring state cannot erase unresolved audio history or reset counters. Hold classification samples actual database time after admission waits, treats missing/legacy history conservatively, and never blocks cleanup itself. Cleanup has separate bounded durable observations/known-conflict attempts; expired claims never imply remote cancellation or mutation permission.
5. **Private one-use ticket and exact replay.** Ticket identity binds the exact manifest/authority/claim/canonical facts and is consumed synchronously before finalization awaits. Failed/unknown finalization cannot reuse it; only the same durable identity/link may reconcile, or another budgeted verification may mint a new ticket. Exact consumed replay is a separate freshly authorized, audit-free outer-settlement branch; partial/unrelated/ineligible state refuses. Both Q registrar reservation refusals and unchanged auth sanitizers remain intact.
6. **Delete/date ordering and WAV projection.** Linked deletion stages head/slots explicitly inside the existing service transaction after its one ledger lock/update, with deferred consistency. No immediate per-ledger trigger may stage heads inside0020's multi-ledger date phase. Date completes ALL ledgers before ANY heads/slots and never takes admission. Worker remains admission->one actual linked ledger->one head->ascending slots, with no later user/student lock. Persisted linked WAV gets a separate closed projection validator for existing page/submit/delete; legacy compressed registration remains unchanged.

The root also resolved the remaining engineering precisions. Head verification consumes one unit of the same foreground claim pool as slot work. The two-buffer limit concerns orchestrator-held/prefetched payloads, with separately bounded C temporaries; it is not a process-wide2MiB assertion. Fixed P/R observers may factor inside the existing private engine with marker/hash/context/time validation, never an arbitrary reader/path/header/token capability. Unknown-B retirement has explicit known R(A) versus verified-B/U-won branches, without fabricating B or using NULL as a comparison escape. Worker maintenance facts carry their own provenance without inventing a human audit actor.

## Implementation acceptance to preserve

The spec provides a concrete executable contract with no unresolved design blocker for this disabled checkpoint. In particular:

- Validate exact-full-cap net-zero consume and unchanged Q new-insert denial, with primary byte/take/ID/marker assertions before/after consume, deleting, physical release and unknown-COMMIT readback.
- Exercise original-ID insertion, reciprocal link, registered transition and one recording.create audit in one transaction, including audit/deferred/freshness rollback, lost ACK, exact replay and genuine uniqueness collision without row adoption.
- Reject forged/NULL/cross-tenant/student/asset/hash/length/frame/duration/link/release combinations through restricted native SQL and deferred final-state checks; prove runtime cannot manufacture release or terminal R evidence.
- Measure both finalizer/date and one-head cleanup orderings, including first absent-ledger insertion. The multi-ledger date barrier must prove an earlier head remains unlocked while date waits on a later ledger; ordinary one-head tests would not catch an immediate row-trigger regression.
- Drive small and maximum28-slot synthetic canonical streams through actual domain transactions and mocked blocked-network C. Verify exact order/hash/EOF/canonical facts, no whole-WAV accumulation, bounded tasks, claim/ticket reuse/staleness, late I/O, context/session/date changes, unknown P/U, finite budgets and fault-hold recovery.
- Preserve durable never-opened possible-P residue, conditional R retirement of opened/ambiguous histories, independently scoped disabled-account maintenance tests and all prior A/B/Q/C regressions. No404/expiry/global quiescence/key reuse/DELETE assertion may substitute for terminal evidence.

The chosen20/25-minute eligibility,60-second claims,3 observation/verification attempts,8/64 fault hold and bounded cleanup tick are initial engineering policy, not a billing cap or proof that remote work stopped. They require implementation tests, not another product decision. Runtime D/normal recording-key acceptance, deployment protection, production migration/worker privilege/schedule, HTTP/client and learner audio remain separate activation work; generated chant remains subject to G3.
