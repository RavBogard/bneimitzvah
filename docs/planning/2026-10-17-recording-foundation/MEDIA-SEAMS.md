## Minimum missing capabilities for the next design

1. **Begin one private capture.** A small same-origin cookie/CSRF-protected action under current own/child `practice-write` must allocate an opaque asset and durable capture intent, bound to tenant/student and rightful capture actor/context. Issue only a short-lived, byte/MIME-bounded, immutable/no-overwrite direct-upload capability. It must grant no read/list/delete. Never accept browser storage paths, provider URLs, family filenames or ownership claims. Current DB/runtime has none of these receipts or provider mappings.
2. **Verify, then register.** Quarantine objects must remain unplayable. Verify object/store identity, signed completion if used, actual bytes/hash/container/codec/duration, limits and receipt binding; do not trust Content-Type, client hash/duration or a completion callback. Decoder work needs bounded memory/time/concurrency and no remote-URL/protocol execution. Consume/transition the intent atomically and recheck live authority/deadline after waits before calling the existing registrar. There is no cross-provider/DB atomic transaction: successful upload plus failed/unknown registration must reconcile without creating another asset or exposing quarantine.
3. **Authorize bytes per request.** Add a separate internal exact-record read under current selected mode, tenant/student, live relationship/context, visible state and deadline; recheck after the storage wait before returning data. Own/child may hear eligible private/submitted; current authorized tutor/admin may hear submitted only; dashboard parent never receives bytes/capabilities. Same-origin delivery must support bounded cancellation/ranges without returning raw provider URLs or credentials, and override caching with private/no-store. Metadata IDs/pagination cursors are not byte capabilities.
4. **Delete and reconcile without user access.** A separately reviewed bounded internal maintenance capability must discover naturally due private/submitted rows, stage deletion under fresh student/recording locks, and process already-deleting rows even after every account/grant is disabled. It needs immutable object mapping, retry/claim state, orphan/expired-intent cleanup and provider-confirmed removal before any trusted ledger-row deletion. Runtime DELETE must remain absent; no public complete-delete or fabricated user audit. A lost delete acknowledgement keeps access denied and reconciles the exact immutable object, rather than reviving or retargeting it.

These are required seams, not proposed final endpoint names or approval to build all four together.

## Consequential decisions and proof gaps

- **Completion identity:** the current registrar requires a live `AccessRequest` plus own/child authority. A provider webhook or background verifier has no such session. The smallest compatible option is background verification plus a fresh, authorized browser finalize action bound to the original capture receipt. Autonomous registration would require a new explicitly reviewed delegation contract; never replay stored cookies or fabricate an actor. Revoked/expired uploads become cleanup work, not playable registrations.
- **Native audio transport:** child practice currently uses `X-Practice-Context`; an ordinary `<audio src>` cannot attach that header. Root must choose a reviewed authenticated fetch/range delivery strategy or a new bounded playback-ticket mechanism. Do not move child handles into query strings or equate a Blob URL with ongoing authorization. Any local object URL/buffer must be stopped/released on concealment, role/child/session change and deadline. Offline playback/leases remain unimplemented.
- **Long streams:** existing authority checks cannot retract bytes already delivered. Decide bounded range/stream lifetime and cancellation/recheck behavior for revocation, deletion or anniversary crossing during playback; do not hold DB authority locks across an unbounded provider stream. Existing post-await checks are reusable, but do not by themselves prove streaming revocation.
- **No resurrection after physical removal:** deleting the ledger row removes its asset-uniqueness protection. Durable consumed/deleted capture receipts and callback fencing must prevent a late completion from recreating it. Choose bounded job/receipt retention deliberately; current0020 makes no permanent tombstone promise.
- **Direct-upload exposure:** provider tokens may remain usable until expiry despite app revocation. Quarantine plus fresh finalization prevents playback, while short validity, per-student/tenant byte/concurrency quotas and orphan sweeping bound storage exposure. Exact quota/TTL/worker budgets remain architecture/cost decisions, not inferred from32MiB per object.
- **Provider feasibility is research, not completed proof:** the existing note recommends private Vercel Blob and direct upload because it records a4.5MB Function payload limit. No Blob SDK/decoder is installed in `package.json`. Pin/recheck the proposed SDK and strict production-only OIDC behavior before implementation; real32MiB upload and large private range/stream delivery need deployed synthetic proof. No Preview/Development permission, static fallback token or new resource/spend approval follows from this inventory.

Before real media, require independent adversarial checks for scoped upload tokens, forged/duplicate/mismatched callbacks, parser/resource limits, fresh authority after upload/verification waits, own/child/staff/parent playback boundaries, range/cache/abort behavior, quota races, orphan/partial-failure reconciliation, disabled-account cleanup, OLD/NEW postponement irreversibility and expired/deleted receipt replay. Use synthetic bytes/local adapters first; later provider/environment tests need the reviewed concrete configuration and unchanged spending/licensing gates.
