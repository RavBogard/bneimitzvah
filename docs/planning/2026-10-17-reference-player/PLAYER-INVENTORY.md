## Exact reusable timing facts and tests

The DTO separates playback `ready/needs-review/unavailable` from word readiness. It exposes an approved opaque asset ID, verse clip start/end, attribution, and one onset per mapped portal word bound to computed NFC `readingTextSha256`; it does not expose paths/archive URLs/index Hebrew. Timings use the **asset** timeline; subtract clip.start only if serving a separately cut clip. Word labels are diagnostic and explicitly forbidden for highlighting. Its 512-word limit is an explicit client safety bound, not a statement about corpus coverage. No Node crypto enters either client module.

`createReferenceTimeline` rejects wrong verse refs and all held/unavailable playback. Word lookup, word loops and word seeks require the independently computed SHA256 of the **actual NFC rendered readingText** plus its projected word count; missing/stale hash or count preserves valid verse playback only. The future UI adapter must compute that digest (never echo the response hash) and fence asynchronous results by current generation. Timeline construction is private and runtime-branded. All seeks, highlights and loop intervals use the original asset's seconds, independent of playback speed; intervals are half-open, the introduction before the first onset has no current word, and the final word ends at the verse clip boundary. Onset gaps are intervals, not invented measured phonetic endings.
