# Draft teaching: V'ahavta (BM p. 11; Shabbat Morning fol. 40–44)

Status: draft 1, voice C. Label until approved: "Written by Claude for CRC · not yet reviewed by Rabbi Daniel Bogard".

## Draft 1

The V'ahavta (Deuteronomy 6:5–9) follows the Sh'ma directly, and the order is the point: having said that all of existence is one, we hear what that asks of us. Love, with all your heart, all your soul and all your might; the rabbis read "might" as everything you have, your means as well as your strength (Mishnah Berakhot 9:5). The paragraph then carries that love into ordinary life: words spoken at home and on the road, at lying down and rising up, bound on the hand and written on the doorposts. Love here is less a feeling than a practice, an obligation to the past, the present and most of all the future. It is a hope that if we live the best of our values, the world will be different.

(About 130 words.)
