# Teaching follow-ups (release 1.1, 2026-10-22)

Three gaps from the final 1.0 audit (STATE, 2026-10-21): tutors had no practice-days view, the congregation's service names/times and consent notice were stored but unused, and the blessings and Sh'ma/V'ahavta were staff picks rather than defaults. Branch `claude/teaching-followups`. No migration.

## Decided here

- **Tutor and admin practice days (MISSION ruling 14).** The existing dates-only read (`GET /api/students/:id/practice-calendar?mode=tutor|admin`) already required the read capability: a live, unrevoked tutor assignment for tutors, any congregation student for admins, and no raw events (`practice-events` stays student-only). No server change. The client calendar now loads that read for tutor and admin views and never reports a visit from them. It shows on the student page's **Lessons** tab under "Practice days", above the lesson log: the week strip with the month calendar behind its disclosure, as the student sees it. It does not go in the header, so the staff header and phone geometry are unchanged. A parent's dashboard keeps its seven-day summary.
- **Service names and times.** The members' settings read (`GET /api/congregation/links`, same route and authority) now also returns `services` (name and start time per slot) and `consentNotice`. Wherever the portal names a student's service, it uses the congregation's name when one is set, otherwise "Morning service" / "Havdalah service". This covers the reading header, the honors sheet and its print copy, My service, and the staff verse editor, which had "(10:00)" / "(4:00)" hard-coded. Start times show only when set, as "9:30 AM". In the reading header the time shows on screens wider than 640px only, so the name line does not wrap on a phone. One read serves the page and is reused for 60 seconds. A failed read is not reused, and saving settings clears it.
- **Consent notice.** PLAN-REVIEW ruling 49 and §5 place it as a line on the parent view. It now shows as the last line of the parent's "Preparation summary" when the setting is filled. When it is blank, nothing shows: no default wording was written (see open items).
- **Settings page.** Removed the "Saved for your records…" lines. The services and consent fields now say where they appear and what blank does. The service-name placeholders read "Morning service" / "Havdalah service".
- **Standard service parts (MISSION 28–30).** The feed's stable ids for the Torah blessings before and after, the haftarah blessings before and after, the Sh'ma and the V'ahavta exist in both families (`…@bm-shacharit`, `…@bm-maariv`). They are listed by id in `DEFAULT_SERVICE_PARTS` (`src/server/service-learning/feed.ts`); only ids are recorded, no text. When a student has **no plan at all**, the staff catalog offers those the feed provides, and the editor starts with them checked and says so. Staff still review and save as before. A saved plan, including an explicitly cleared one, is never preselected over. Nothing is written automatically.

## Open items that wait on Daniel

- **Automatic standard parts.** Today a student sees no service parts until a tutor or admin saves a plan, though the standard parts are now pre-checked. Giving every new student those parts with no staff save would need the portal to author a plan itself. Every plan row and audit event carries the staff member who set it, so the open question is who authors that plan and when (on student creation, when the slot is set, or on first read). Not done without your ruling.
- **Default consent wording.** MISSION's clarification of rulings 39/48–49 asks for a parent-dashboard notice on storage, access and one-year deletion. The portal shows the congregation's own text and nothing when it is blank. Fill it in Settings, or ask for a default wording.
