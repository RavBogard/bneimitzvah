## W2 Singing synthesis (SVS)

SVS = singing voice synthesis: software that turns a score plus lyrics into sung audio. MOS = mean opinion score, listeners rating 1–5; only comparable within one paper.

### OpenVPI DiffSinger and its tooling

- **OpenVPI DiffSinger (repo, licence, docs, config, commits)** — github.com/openvpi/DiffSinger (README, LICENSE, docs/BestPractices.md, docs/GettingStarted.md, configs/templates/config_acoustic.yaml) — read 2026-10-22; commits 2026-09-15 to 2026-10-16 — The best fit for training a Hebrew voice on our own data: takes phones, durations in seconds and a pitch curve; supports a custom phone dictionary, two-speaker training and fine-tuning; very active. — Primary, verified (licence and code read) — Apache-2.0.
- **DiffSinger releases** — github.com/openvpi/DiffSinger/releases — v2.5.1 2026-01-08; v2.5.0 2025-04-01; v2.4.0 2024-07-13; v2.3.0 2024-03-11 — Multilingual dictionary refactor and a language-embedding bug fix, LYNXNet backbone, Rectified Flow (a faster diffusion method). — Primary, verified.
- **MakeDiffSinger (data-preparation pipelines)** — github.com/openvpi/MakeDiffSinger (README, LICENSE); https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openvpi/MakeDiffSinger/main/acoustic_forced_alignment/README.md and …/variance-temp-solution/README.md — read 2026-10-22 (current main) — Recording rules (above 32 kHz, no noise or reverb, 5–15 s segments), at least 20 examples of each phone, and the label types needed. — Primary, verified — BSD-3-Clause.
- **SOME (note extraction from singing)** — github.com/openvpi/SOME — v1.0.0-baseline 2024-01-19 — Extracts MIDI notes from sung audio; needed only if notes are not taken from the trope engine. — Primary, verified — MIT.
- **OpenUtau** — github.com/stakira/OpenUtau releases; github.com/openutau/OpenUtau/wiki/Voicebank-development; https://github.com/stakira/OpenUtau/wiki/Voicebank-development — releases 2026-10-20, -21, -22 — Free singing editor that runs DiffSinger voices; describes UTAU-style sample splicing; no Hebrew phonemizer listed. — Primary, verified (W2) — MIT (licence not checked in W8).
- **DiffTrainer (community training GUI)** — github.com/agentasteriski/DiffTrainer README — pushed 2026-10-20 — Bundles SOME, DiffSinger training and OpenUtau export. — Primary, claimed (README) — no licence file found.
- **ADCx SF 2023 talk, "Creating your own singing voice synthesizer"** — data.audio.dev/talks/ADCxSF/2023/creating-your-own-singing-voice-synthesizer/slides.pdf (also given as https://data.audio.dev/talks/ADCxSF/2023/creating-your-own-singing-voice-synthesizer/slides.pdf) — 2023-05-15 — Community rule of thumb: DiffSinger needs 1–8 h of data and about 8 GPU-hours; NNSVS 1–2 h. — Secondary (conference talk, older code).

## W2 Voice conversion, pitch tools, vocoders

SVC = singing voice conversion: re-voices a recording in another timbre along a supplied pitch line. F0 = the pitch track. Vocoder = the component that turns the model's sound picture into audio.
