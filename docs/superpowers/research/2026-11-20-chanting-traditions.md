# Complete Torah recordings from other traditions

CRC listening research, November 20, 2026. A portion count below measures the published catalog. A successful sample check confirms that audio was available for that selection. Each tradition follows its own reading divisions and haftarah cycle.

| Tradition and reader | What the publisher supplies | Listen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moroccan · Natanel Chen Ohana | Ta’ama has 54 portion pages with nine audio players apiece, covering the seven aliyot, maftir and haftarah. The opening aliyah of the first and last portions both served MP3 audio. The project also uses violin accompaniment. | [Bereshit, first aliyah](https://tht.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/קריאת-פרשת-בראשית-1.mp3) · [Full Bereshit page](https://tht.co.il/פרשת-בראשית/) · [About Ta’ama](https://tht.co.il/אודות/) |
| Damascus Syrian · Faraj Samra | The five book indexes have 54 whole-portion recordings, 378 individual aliyot and 54 haftarah entries. The first Torah and haftarah samples served MP3 audio. His existing media server works over HTTP. CRC would need an HTTPS source or permission to host its own copy. | [Bereshit with aliyot and haftarah](http://www.sephardichazzanut.com/Bereshit.htm) · [Samra’s account and contact](http://www.sephardichazzanut.com/me.htm) |
| London Spanish and Portuguese · Hazzan Daniel Halfon | Heshaim commissioned this set for synagogue preparation. The five indexes cover all 54 portions in 381 MP3 links. The opening Genesis recording served playable audio. The commissioned collection documents Torah coverage. | [Bereshit, first reading](https://www.spmusic.org/daniel_halfon_perashah/bereshit_1.mp3) · [Congregation’s introduction](https://www.sephardi.org.uk/community/sephardi-music/sephardi-music-perasha/) · [Five-book archive](https://sites.google.com/site/londonsephardiperasha/) |
| Yemenite Baladi · Amir Tzabari | Nosach Teiman’s USB edition explicitly includes his complete Torah and haftarot. The website indexes all 54 portions. Three accessible portion pages each had eight recordings, and Ki Tisa’s opening MP3 passed a media check. Several other pages returned server errors. | [Ki Tisa, first aliyah](https://nosachteiman.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/פרשת-כי-תשא-ראשון.mp3) · [Full edition](https://nosachteiman.co.il/shop/קריאת-התורה-אמיר-צברי/) · [Publisher terms](https://nosachteiman.co.il/תנאי-שימוש/) |
| Italian · Moise Levy and Davide Muggia | Levy’s introduction documents two complete Italian-rite Torah performances, one by each reader, distributed with the multimedia edition. The public almanac offers a Levy sample. A complete haftarah audio cycle by each reader is unconfirmed. | [Levy’s almanac and audio](https://libri.levy.it/almanacco/) · [Publisher’s introduction, DVD section](https://libri.levy.it/esempi/ToraRashi/ToraRashi.pdf) · [Edizioni Levy](https://www.edizionilevy.it/) |

**Another Syrian voice for a direct comparison.** The Sephardic Pizmonim Project lists an Abe Zami performance marked “Full” for each of the 54 portions. Its haftarah collection features several readers. [Zami’s Bereshit](https://pizmonim.org/book.php?recording=3054) · [Torah index](https://pizmonim.org/section.php?maqam=Pentateuch) · [Haftarah index](https://pizmonim.org/section.php?maqam=Haftarot).

For CRC, Ohana and Samra offer the strongest documented coverage for practicing one assigned aliyah plus its haftarah. Halfon supplies a third voice for an opening-Genesis listening comparison. None of the sources above showed a grant covering both third-party public streaming and device downloads. An agreement should specify attribution, public and shared reading links, offline copies, and permission to make verse cuts if CRC wants tap-to-play. No publisher has been contacted.
